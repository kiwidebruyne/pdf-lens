# Translation and source review

The goal is to help someone read the source carefully while keeping the original PDF visible and authoritative. Translate what the text says; do not replace close reading with a summary or teach extra material that is absent from the source.

## Natural translation

Write one fluent Korean translation for each complete sentence, preserving the source claim. Preserve negation, conditions (especially only/if/unless), comparison bases, quantities, units, tense, modality and uncertainty. Do not turn association into causation, add explanation, fill a missing premise or solve an exercise.

## Word cards

Each English prose token has a basic meaning (`base`) and concise contextual meaning (`meaning`). Resolve polysemy from the complete sentence and surrounding example. For function words preserve the relevant sense without adding grammatical-role descriptions. Keep recognized technical terms and symbol case consistent using a small author reference note. Do not author POS, role, expressions or shared dictionary entries. Names can be retained or transliterated; no cards are needed for numbers, punctuation or formula syntax.

## Source check during writing

Read complete sentences and examples, and check formula crops and anomalous glyphs during the same authoring pass. Check substantive conditions, negation, symbol identity and page boundaries before finishing your assigned fragment. Do not add a separate full language-review model pass. Structural validation cannot establish semantic correctness.
