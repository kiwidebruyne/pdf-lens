# Translation and source review

The goal is to help someone read the source carefully while keeping the original PDF visible and authoritative. Translate what the text says; do not replace close reading with a summary or teach extra material that is absent from the source.

## Two Korean renderings

The default literal rendering follows the English phrase and clause order. Divide it into meaningful chunks, not word-by-word fragments; the reader joins chunks with ` / `. The natural rendering uses fluent Korean syntax while preserving the same claim and information.

For example, the source sentence “The method is reliable only when the data represent the target population.” can be rendered literally as “그 방법은 신뢰할 수 있다 / 오직 데이터가 목표 모집단을 대표할 때에만.” A natural rendering is “그 방법은 데이터가 목표 모집단을 대표하는 경우에만 신뢰할 수 있다.” Neither rendering may weaken `only` or omit the condition.

Preserve negation, conditions, comparison bases, quantities, units, tense, modality and uncertainty (`may`, `suggests`, `approximately`). Keep ambiguity when the source is ambiguous. Do not turn association into causation, add an explanation, fill a missing premise, or present a conclusion the authors did not make. Flag only substantive source ambiguity that remains after reading nearby context.

## Word cards

Every included English prose token gets a contextual word card. Reuse a base lexicon entry only when lemma, part of speech and core sense match. The card's contextual meaning and role describe this occurrence. Give function words meaningful roles too: for example, distinguish a relative-pronoun `that` from a complementizer, comparison `as` from causal `as`, and statistical `significant` from ordinary importance. Explain a multiword technical term together when separate word glosses would mislead.

Do not use raw English, placeholder text or a dictionary's first meaning as a contextual explanation. Names may be retained or transliterated; recognized technical terms can keep English in parentheses. Do not fabricate dictionary entries for numbers, punctuation or mathematical syntax. A line-break join is justified only when the rendered page confirms that the split tokens form one word; keep the original source tokens and positions.

## Final language pass

Compare the annotations against the rendered source and check the two modes independently. Focus on conditions, negation, comparison, units, uncertain claims, sentence boundaries, equation references and words whose role depends on context. Structural validity is necessary but does not prove faithful translation.
